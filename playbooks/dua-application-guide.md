# DUA Application Guide

Use this checklist for ResDAC/CMS VRDC, SEER-Medicare, T-MSIS, dbGaP, UK Biobank, ADNI, and similar governed assets.

1. State the exact research question and intended analytic methods.
2. Identify minimum necessary files, years, geography, and identifiers.
3. Document PHI handling, access controls, storage location, retention, and destruction plan.
4. Prepare IRB determination or approval when required.
5. Budget for fees, secure enclave/cloud costs, and legal review.
6. Build and validate ETL on open/synthetic surrogates before protected data arrive.
