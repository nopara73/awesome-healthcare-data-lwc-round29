# Synthetic Prototyping

Recommended progression:

1. Use DE-SynPUF to test claims schemas, joins, and CI.
2. Use Synthea or project-specific synthetic data for EHR-style experiments.
3. Use public microdata such as NHANES/MEPS for survey-weighted model scaffolding.
4. Move to governed data only after feature definitions, leakage checks, and model cards are drafted.

Never treat synthetic data as clinical truth unless the generator and validation study support that claim.
