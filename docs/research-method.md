# Research method

This scaffold follows the six-attribute diligence pattern used in the source research:

1. Access tier and licensing.
2. Grain, schema, and identifiers.
3. Coverage, size, and representativeness.
4. PHI, re-identification risk, and de-identification method.
5. Known quality issues and analytical gotchas.
6. Canonical ETL path, including OMOP/PCORnet mapping status.

Entries marked `reference` contain extra acquisition and ML guidance. Entries marked `seed` are schema-complete and intentionally ready for community review.
