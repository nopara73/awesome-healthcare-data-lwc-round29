# PHI and re-identification guide

The catalog records PHI posture, but it is not legal advice. Treat these as acquisition-planning signals:

- Open aggregate datasets can still create small-cell disclosure risk after joins.
- De-identified clinical and imaging datasets can retain re-identification risk through dates, rare conditions, longitudinal detail, images, or genomics.
- Limited Data Sets and Research Identifiable Files require policy, technical, and contractual controls.
- Model outputs can leak protected facts; validate suppression rules for dashboards and reports.
